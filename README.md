# Pixel Cafe

## Description
This is a project for the Order Up Quest for Hackclub Pixl. You start with a randomly generated budget and you try to spend it all on donuts and drinks at the Pixel Cafe. My main goals for this website was to learn Astro and Tailwind(this is my first time using both) and get more comfortable with JS.

## How it works 
1. Roll for your budget. This is the unique part of this cafe website. The budget ranges from $10 to $100, and it is how much money you have to spend at the cafe. 
2. Pick your items. You can go between the donuts tab and the drinks tab to put the items you want in your cart. It tells you your budget and the total value of the items currently in your cart at the top. 
3. Make it fit your budget. You cannot checkout if the items in your cart exceeds your budget, so you have to manage your cart well to get everything you want. Click on the cart icon and add/remove items to fit within your budget
4. Checkout and get a receipt!
